# Physiyoga Logo Design: Clinical Refinement

Based on your feedback, I've refined the logo proposals to focus more on the **clinical/medical authority** of the brand while moving away from any overt "Yoga" imagery that might cause confusion.

The focus is now squarely on the **Rod of Asclepius** and professional medical-grade typography. 

Any subtitle used has been shifted to your core principle: **"Movement is Medicine."**

---

## Refined Concept A: Clinical Fusion (The Medical Mark)
This design uses a sharp, modern rendition of the Rod of Asclepius. It’s professional, authoritative, and reinforces your standing as a clinical physiotherapist. It removes the "softness" of the previous versions to look strictly medical.

## Refined Concept B: Geometric Initial (The Modern Emblem)
This version integrates the medical emblem into a stylized, geometric 'P'. It’s an abstracted, modern seal that feels permanent and solid—perfect for business stationary or a medical clinic’s front door.

### Key Adjustments:
1.  **Removed Yoga Imagery:** No postures, fluid shapes, or lotus-style icons. The focus is purely on the Rod of Asclepius.
2.  **Subtitle Update:** If used, the subtitle is now exclusively **"Movement is Medicine."**
3.  **Typography:** Used bold, sharp sans-serif (based on Outfit and Inter) to emphasize the clinical aspect.
4.  **Color Integrity:** Maintained the charcoal ($#272a31$) and gold ($#FF9500$) for total brand consistency.
