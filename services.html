Our Services at Physiyoga

At Physiyoga, we provide musculoskeletal physiotherapy and movement-based rehabilitation rooted in biomechanics, tissue loading principles, and functional movement science. We focus on identifying why pain or injury developed, not only where symptoms are felt.

Movement methods inspired by Ashtanga Yoga are used only as therapeutic tools, guided entirely by physiotherapy assessment and clinical reasoning.

Our Clinical Approach

  • ✓ Musculoskeletal focus only
  • ✓ Assessment-driven decision making
  • ✓ One-to-one physiotherapy sessions
  • ✓ Active rehabilitation over passive dependency
  • ✓ Emphasis on long-term movement efficiency and recurrence prevention

In simple terms:

We analyse how you move.

We correct what overloads your body.

We help you return to activity — safely and confidently.
